Green Room

Green Room studio

Write a post, check how it will look, then copy the updated file. Nothing is sent or saved anywhere.

1. Write

Blank line = new paragraph. ## Heading for a sub-heading, --- for a divider, *italic* and **bold** inside a line. Poems keep their line breaks.

2. Preview

This is how your post will look in the Green Room.

3. Publish

  1. Press Build posts.js, then Copy.
  2. In your site folder, open assets/posts.js, select everything, paste, save.
  3. Upload the folder to Netlify again. The post appears in the Green Room and on the home page.

To remove or edit an old post, change its block in assets/posts.js directly. The build runs a check, so a typo stops the deploy instead of breaking the page.
