The Story

She built the most trusted man in Nollywood. Now someone is tearing down everyone who ever doubted him.

For eleven years, Morayo Adekunle has been the one thing standing between Folarin Ajayi and disaster: his memory, his shield, the person who knows exactly which truths never leave the room. Then the critics who once wrote him off start dying, each death staged with a precision that reads less like rage and more like a scripted revenge.

A detective who doesn't believe in coincidence starts pulling every thread in Folarin's world. And the closer she gets, the clearer it becomes: the woman who's spent a decade protecting him has been hiding something far more dangerous than his secrets.

Faith, fame, and power collide in a story about what happens when devotion curdles into control, and control curdles into something far worse.

Perfect for readers who like their thrillers psychological and their power plays personal.
